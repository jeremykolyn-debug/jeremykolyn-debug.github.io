Key terms used across the units, in alphabetical order. Link to a letter section from any page with `[term](/glossary/#a)`.

## A

Abstract
: A short summary at the start of an academic paper describing its purpose, methods, findings, and conclusions.

## C

Citation
: A reference to a source, giving enough information for a reader to find it.

## P

Peer review
: The process in which experts evaluate a piece of research before it is published.
