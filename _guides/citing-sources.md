## Why Cite?

Citations credit other people's work, let readers check your sources, and protect you from plagiarism.

## Common Styles

| Style | Common in | Book example |
|-------|-----------|--------------|
| APA   | Social sciences | Author, A. (Year). *Title*. Publisher. |
| MLA   | Humanities | Author. *Title*. Publisher, Year. |
| Chicago | History | Author. *Title*. City: Publisher, Year. |

## Tools

Reference managers such as Zotero can store sources and format citations automatically.
