## The Three-Pass Method

1. **Skim (5–10 min):** title, abstract, headings, figures, conclusion. Decide if it's relevant.
2. **Read (about 1 hour):** the whole paper, noting key claims and anything you don't understand.
3. **Analyze:** question the methods and assumptions; compare with other sources.

## Questions to Ask

- What problem does the paper address?
- What evidence supports its claims?
- What are its limitations?
